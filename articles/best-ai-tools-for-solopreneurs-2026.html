1. Systeme.io — the all-in-one option

Systeme.io bundles the tools most solopreneurs end up paying for separately: landing pages, sales funnels, email marketing, a course platform, and basic affiliate management. The pitch is straightforward — instead of stitching together five subscriptions and hoping they talk to each other, you run everything from one dashboard.

The trade-off is that no individual piece is best-in-class. The email editor isn't as flexible as a dedicated email tool, and the page builder isn't as slick as a dedicated funnel builder. What you get instead is one login, one price, and no integration headaches. For someone just getting a digital product or service off the ground, that trade is usually worth it.

Systeme.io has a genuinely usable free plan, which makes it a reasonable place to test whether a funnel-based sales process even fits your business before paying for anything.

2. Jasper AI — for high-volume written content

Jasper is a dedicated AI writing tool built around marketing use cases: blog posts, ad copy, product descriptions, email campaigns. It's more structured than a general-purpose chatbot — it comes with brand-voice settings, campaign templates, and workflows meant to keep output consistent across a lot of content.

Where Jasper earns its price tag is volume and consistency. If you're writing the occasional blog post, ChatGPT will get you most of the way there for free. If you're producing content daily across multiple formats and want it to sound like the same brand every time, Jasper's structure starts to pay for itself. It's not a fit for someone who writes rarely, or who's happy hand-tuning prompts in a general chatbot.

Who should skip it

If your content output is low-volume or occasional, the subscription cost is hard to justify over a general-purpose AI assistant you're likely already paying for.

3. Kit — email marketing for creators

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) is built specifically for people who make money from an audience — newsletters, digital products, paid communities. Compared to general marketing platforms, it's opinionated toward creators: simple automation ("if someone buys X, tag them and stop the welcome sequence"), a built-in landing page/opt-in builder, and reporting that focuses on subscriber growth and revenue per email rather than generic marketing metrics.

If your entire business model is "grow a list, sell to that list," Kit is one of the more purpose-built tools for that specific loop. If you need email as one small piece of a bigger funnel — landing pages, courses, affiliates — an all-in-one tool like Systeme.io may cover the same need with less total cost.

4. Descript — edit audio and video like a text document

Descript's core idea is simple and genuinely useful: it transcribes your recording, and you edit the transcript instead of a waveform or timeline. Delete a sentence in the text, and it deletes that clip from the audio or video. For anyone who didn't come up as a video editor, that's a much lower barrier than a traditional editing tool.

It also includes AI filler-word removal, studio-quality voice cleanup, and overdub features for fixing small mistakes without a re-record. For solo podcasters and course creators, this can turn editing from a multi-hour task into something done in the time it takes to read through a transcript.

5. Opus Clip — turn long video into short clips automatically

If you already record long-form video — podcasts, webinars, livestreams — Opus Clip's job is to find the moments worth clipping and turn them into short, captioned, vertical videos for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. It uses AI to rank segments by engagement potential, rather than requiring you to scrub through an hour of footage yourself.

It's a repurposing tool, not a content-creation tool — it needs a long-form source to work from. If you don't already record long-form, this isn't the place to start; a tool like this earns its keep once you have a backlog of recordings you're not doing anything with.

How to actually choose

Ignore the demos and look at your own week instead. What's the thing you keep putting off, or paying someone else to do badly?

Selling something through a funnel, start with Systeme.io. Writing marketing copy every day, Jasper earns its keep. If your whole business is basically "grow a list, sell to that list," Kit was built for exactly that loop. Dread editing your own podcast or video? Descript fixes that. And if you've already got a stack of long recordings doing nothing, Opus Clip turns them into something.

Pick the one that removes your actual bottleneck this month. The rest can wait.

FAQ

Do I need all five of these tools?
No. Each one solves a different problem. Most one-person businesses only need one or two of these at any given stage.
What's the cheapest way to start?
Systeme.io has a free plan that covers funnels, email, and a basic course area, which makes it a reasonable starting point if you're not yet sure a paid stack is worth it.
Are these tools a replacement for ChatGPT?
No — they solve different problems. ChatGPT is a general assistant; these are purpose-built for a specific job (funnels, writing volume, email, editing, or clipping).
