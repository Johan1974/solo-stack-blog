Systeme.io vs Kit: Which One Should Solopreneurs Actually Use?

Systeme.io vs Kit comes up constantly in solopreneur circles because both tools solve the "how do I actually sell something" problem, just from opposite directions. Systeme.io wants to be your whole sales stack. Kit wants to be the best possible tool for one piece of it: your email list. Neither pitch is wrong, which is exactly why the choice is confusing.

The short version

If email is one part of a bigger funnel you still need to build — landing pages, checkout, maybe a course — Systeme.io covers all of it for one subscription. If your business already runs on a newsletter and email is the whole game, not a supporting piece, Kit is built specifically for that and does it better than Systeme.io's built-in email tools ever will. Read on if you want the reasoning, not just the answer.

What each tool is actually built to do

Systeme.io is an all-in-one platform: funnels, landing pages, email, a course area, and a basic affiliate manager, all under one login and one price. The email tool inside it is genuinely usable, but it's one feature among several, not the whole product. I covered the full picture in my Systeme.io review if you want the longer version.

Kit — formerly ConvertKit — does the opposite. It doesn't touch funnels, checkout, or courses at all. What it does is build a visual automation system around exactly how creators sell to an audience: tag someone based on what they click, move them into a sequence, report on which email actually drove revenue. I go deeper on that in the Kit review.

Pricing, in plain terms

Systeme.io's paid tiers unlock more contacts, more funnels, and features like A/B testing, but the free plan alone is enough to launch something real. Kit's pricing scales with how many subscribers you have rather than how much you send, so a large but half-dead list can cost more than a smaller, engaged one. Both companies adjust limits and prices over time, so check current numbers on their sites before you commit rather than trusting a figure printed in an article.

Where Systeme.io wins

Anywhere you need more than email. If you're starting from zero and need a landing page, an email sequence, and a checkout page before you've made a sale, paying for three separate tools and wiring them together is a waste of a weekend. Systeme.io's whole reason for existing is to remove that integration work, and for a first product, that's usually worth more than best-in-class email.

Where Kit wins

Once your business actually runs on an audience — a newsletter people look forward to, a paid community, a steady stream of digital product sales through email — Kit's automation and reporting start to matter in a way Systeme.io's more general email tool doesn't match. It's not trying to also be a funnel builder, and that focus shows up in how the tool is designed to think about subscribers.

Who should pick Systeme.io

  • You don't have a funnel, checkout, or email system in place yet and want one tool instead of three.
  • You want to test whether a sales funnel even works for your product before spending money on anything.
  • Courses or a paid community are part of the plan, not just email.

Who should pick Kit

  • Your business already runs on a newsletter or an audience, and email is the main channel, not a supporting piece.
  • You want automation and reporting built around creator-style selling rather than generic marketing metrics.
  • You already have funnels and checkout sorted elsewhere and just need better email.

My take

I'd start with Systeme.io if you're not sure yet — the free plan means testing it costs nothing but time, and it covers the gaps a solo operation usually has (no funnel, no checkout, no course area). Move to Kit later, specifically for email, once you can point at a real reason: your list is growing, email is driving actual sales, and Systeme.io's automation is starting to feel limiting. Buying the specialized tool before you've outgrown the general one is how a lot of solopreneurs end up paying for software they don't need yet.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io better than Kit?
It depends what you're weighing it against. For email marketing specifically, Kit is the more purpose-built tool. If you also need funnels, checkout pages and course hosting, Systeme.io covers more ground for one price.
Can I use Systeme.io and Kit together?
Technically yes, but for most solopreneurs it defeats the point — you'd be paying for email twice. It makes more sense if you've outgrown Systeme.io's email tools specifically but still want to keep its funnels and course area.
Which has a better free plan, Systeme.io or Kit?
Systeme.io's free plan covers more ground (funnels, email and a basic course area), while Kit's free plan is narrower but focused entirely on email. Which is "better" depends on whether you need the extra pieces.
Do I need Kit if I already use Systeme.io?
Not necessarily. Systeme.io's email tools are enough for most solopreneurs starting out. Switch to Kit only once you've outgrown what Systeme.io's automation can do and email has become your main channel.
